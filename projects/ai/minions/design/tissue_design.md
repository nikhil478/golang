Minion is a state-transition orchestrator with AI capabilities, designed to move work from one state to another.

What does Minion comprise of?

A sandbox: an isolated environment where the agent can build its knowledge, perform actions, and operate without requiring human intervention.
An agent: a selectable AI agent with different capabilities.
A toolbox: a collection of tools that the agent may need during execution.


Out of scope:

Sandbox: secret providers, integrations with cloud platforms such as AWS, GCP, and Oracle.
Agent: multi-agent capabilities, support for paid or open-source agents, and workflows with a defined schema.
Observability capabilities of the agent
